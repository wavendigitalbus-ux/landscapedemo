Lawn Mowing & Maintenance

Regular mowing, precision line trimming, vertical driveway edging, and hardscape blowing tailored to keep Florida turf healthy, dense, and neatly manicured.

We tailor our cutting schedules to Florida's growth seasons—weekly through summer's heat and rain, and bi-weekly during cooler winter months—ensuring your turf remains green without scalping or weed stress.

  • Precise Vertical Edging Razor-sharp separation between lawn, concrete walkways, driveways, and curbs.
  • Clean Hardscape Blowing Driveways, walkways, patios, and pool decks blown clear of grass clippings every time.

TURF RESTORATION

Sod Installation

Refresh worn-out, weed-infested, or damaged lawns with professionally installed sod. A fresh turf installation instantly revives your property's value and provides a lush, thick carpet of green grass.

We manage the entire process properly: clearing out dying grass, grading soil for proper drainage, and tightly seaming premium farm-fresh sod rolls for rapid root establishment.

  • Substrate Preparation & Grading Old lawn removal and careful leveling so water flows naturally away from foundations.
  • Florida Sod Varieties Options include St. Augustine (Floratam / Seville), Empire Zoysia, and Bahia.
