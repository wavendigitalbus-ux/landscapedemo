HAVE QUESTIONS?

Common Questions Before Getting Started

Everything you need to know about our quote process, scheduling, and client guarantees.

Do I need to be home when you inspect or mow? expand_more

No. As long as our crews have gate access and pets are kept indoors, we can perform on-site yard assessments and scheduled maintenance visits while you are at work. We will send an SMS or email notification once service is finished.

Are there long-term contracts? expand_more

Never. All our recurring mowing, trimming, and grounds maintenance agreements are month-to-month. We take immense pride in earning your business with every single visit and you can modify or pause service at any time with straightforward notice.

What grass types do you specialize in? expand_more

We specialize specifically in Central Florida warm-season turf species, including St. Augustine (Floratam, Seville, and Palmetto), Empire Zoysia, Bahia, and Bermuda. Our mowing decks are set precisely to the recommended horticulturist heights for each variety to prevent root scalping and promote pest resistance.

How quickly can service begin after quote approval? expand_more

Most recurring maintenance routes can start within 3 to 5 business days of proposal acceptance. For major landscape renovations, sod installation, or storm cleanup projects, we reserve specific crew calendar days based on current material deliveries and weather forecasts.
